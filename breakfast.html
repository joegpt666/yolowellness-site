Breakfast at YOLO — fresh, local, easy.

Wake up slow; we’ve got the good stuff. Fresh-from-the-market produce, small-batch hot dishes, and happy carbs to kickstart your day.

Locally sourced · Made this morning

What makes it special?

  • Local & seasonal — greens and fruit picked nearby, rotating with the market.
  • Cooked fresh — small batches, no overnight stuff. Warm dishes stay warm, salads stay crisp.
  • Energizing balance — grains, protein, and colorful veg so you feel light but fueled.
  • Grab-and-go friendly — dine in or take it on the way to Asok MRT.

  • House-made morning congee with daily toppings (ginger, scallion, sesame oil).
